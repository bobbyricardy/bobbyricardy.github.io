About Me

Full-Stack & Observability Engineer · Singapore

I'm a full-stack engineer with a background in banking — mostly backend systems, data pipelines, and internal tools. Over seven years, I've designed and scaled systems end-to-end, from Kafka and Elasticsearch ingestion layers to React web applications and custom operational dashboards.

Over time I moved into leading engineering teams, including as Vice President at DBS Bank, where I led work standardizing multi-market telemetry and building centralized observability platforms tracking latency, availability, and service health bank-wide.

My primary toolkit includes JavaScript, React, Node.js, Python, Java, Apache Kafka, Elasticsearch, and Linux.
